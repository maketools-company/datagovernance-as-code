| Term | Domain | Definition | Status |

| Active customer | Customer | A customer with at least one transaction initiated by them in the last 90 days. | approved |

## Policies

| Policy | Applies to | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal data is not kept longer than needed**. Personal data is deleted or anonymised 5 years after the customer relationship ends, unless a law requires a longer period. | Personal data, Special category personal data | Data Protection Officer |
| **Confidential data is shared only through data contracts**. Consumers read confidential and personal data through the output port of a data product with an active contract, never from raw tables. | Confidential, Personal data, Special category personal data | Data Owner |
| **No automated decision about a customer without human review**. Scores and AI outputs about a customer may support a decision, but a person makes it. | Personal data | Data Protection Officer |
